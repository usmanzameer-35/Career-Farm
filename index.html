Frequently Asked Questions

What is a CV website?

A CV website is a personal professional website that presents your experience, education, skills, achievements and career story online.

Can you turn my CV into a website?

Yes. Careers Farm uses your existing CV or resume as the starting material for your personal website.

What is a resume portfolio website?

It combines resume information with richer portfolio content such as projects, research, publications, achievements and interactive career storytelling.

What professions does Careers Farm support?

Careers Farm designs for professionals across industries and disciplines rather than a fixed list of occupations.

Can you build an executive personal website?

Yes. The Executive offer includes career positioning, executive-level copywriting and leadership-focused personal branding.

Will my portfolio work on mobile?

Yes. Careers Farm websites are designed to work across desktop and mobile screens.

Can I connect my own domain?

Yes. Custom-domain setup is included with the Executive offer; the domain itself is purchased separately by the client.
